Rio has a generous geography. Hills in the middle of the city, the sea on one side, the forest on the other. It means there is almost always a viewpoint nearby, and that the landscape takes care of itself at any hour of the day. These are the five I use most.

## Arpoador

The rock at the far end of Ipanema is the most carioca viewpoint there is. From the top you see the whole of Ipanema on one side, Copacabana on the other, and the Dois Irmãos peaks closing the horizon. It is a quick climb and it sits on the way of almost every itinerary in the south zone. In the morning there are fewer people, in the afternoon there is more life. Both versions are worth the stop.

## Sugarloaf Mountain

Two cable cars and two different landscapes. From the Morro da Urca station you look out over Botafogo and Guanabara Bay. From the top, the whole city opens up. It is the trip I recommend most to people who are short on time, because in two hours you understand how Rio is put together. What makes the difference up there is not the hour, it is a clear sky.

## Mirante Dona Marta

Here you see Christ the Redeemer on one side and Guanabara Bay on the other, with the entire city in between. It is one of the few places where Rio fits into a single photograph. It sits high, the visit is quick, and it slots in nicely before or after anything else.

## Mirante do Leblon

The left hand end of Leblon has a small viewpoint, almost always less crowded than Arpoador, with the same view of the Dois Irmãos. I take people there when they want the landscape at an easy pace, without the crowd.

## Rodrigo de Freitas Lagoon

The lagoon is a different kind of plan. The water mirrors Corcovado and the hills around it, the ground is flat, and you can walk or sit at a kiosk. It works well with children, with anyone who has already walked a lot, and with anyone who simply wants to look at the city without rushing.

## How to choose

If you are short on time, Sugarloaf. If you want the Rio of the locals, Arpoador. If you want the photograph that explains the city, Mirante Dona Marta. If you want quiet, Leblon. If you want to sit down with a drink, the lagoon.

You do not need to build your trip around a particular hour. I plan the route around the time you have and fit in whichever viewpoint makes the most sense along the way.
